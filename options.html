Bravely

A visual browsing companion to Pinfull. These switches control the look on each site.

Optional access

Both switches start off. Enable one only if you want the extra features. Your browser will ask you to approve access.

Allows access to other HTTPS websites for full-size image discovery and Lens uploads. With this off, supported sites and their image servers still work; other results may keep their thumbnail. Allows image requests to localhost and 127.0.0.1 over HTTP, for apps running on this computer. Ordinary Pinfull links to Pinterest images do not need this.

These switches apply immediately and are specific to this installation. Switching off revokes the optional access. Basic access to supported sites is required for Bravely's normal features.
Ctrl+Shift+X on Pinterest switches it too. Open Pinterest tabs reload when it changes.
Ctrl+Shift+X on DeviantArt switches it too. Open DeviantArt tabs reload when it changes.
The red strip always opens the full-size picture.
Ctrl+Shift+X on pixiv switches it too. Open pixiv tabs reload when it changes.
The red strip always opens the original picture.
